Preparation plan

What to study for each track, in what order, and how to use this site to do it

The plan follows two facts from the report: the three tracks ask for different skills, and interview loops test a narrower set than postings list. Pick the track you are applying to, work the areas in its order, and let the review app decide what to revisit.

The daily loop

  1. Review, 30 to 40 minutes. Open Review, set your track, and clear what is due plus the day’s new cards. Answer out loud before revealing. Grade honestly: “Good” only if you would have said it in an interview.
  2. One design case every second day. Take a case from Design cases, give yourself 35 minutes on paper, then compare with the answer and its follow-ups.
  3. Coding, 45 minutes. Two problems in the patterns listed in Software engineering, or one ML-coding problem from Deep learning written from a blank file.
  4. One story a day in the last two weeks. Write one behavioural story against the prompts in Senior scope and say it in under three minutes.

The Progress tab of the review app shows a readiness figure per track: the share of cards you know, weighted by how often that track’s postings name each skill. It also lists the highest-demand skills you have not yet covered, with a button to drill each one. Use that list, not this page, once you have started.

Six weeks

A schedule for about ten hours a week. Compress it by dropping the awareness areas.

Week MLE AI engineer Research engineer
1 ML fundamentals; coding patterns LLM applications: agents, tools, context; coding patterns Deep learning; ML coding from a blank file
2 Deep learning; ML coding LLM applications: RAG, guardrails, cost; backend and API design LLM training; reinforcement learning
3 Search and recsys, or the domain of your target companies Evaluation; MLOps and cloud Training infrastructure; evaluation and experiment design
4 Evaluation; inference; MLOps Inference; system design; design cases 6 to 10 Research skills; paper discussion; inference
5 Design cases 2 to 5 and 11 to 16; LLM training and applications at working depth Design cases; LLM training at working depth; a small agent built end to end Design cases 9 to 11 and 15; LLM applications at working depth
6 Senior scope: project deep dive and stories; mock loops Senior scope; customer scenarios; mock loops Senior scope; research deep dive; mock loops

In the last week use Mock interview in the review app: one full loop on two separate days, and the round types you scored worst on in between.

Choosing where to apply

Open roles lists the postings behind the report. Filter by role type first: the eight role types in the report ask for different things under the same title, and a posting’s skill tags show at a glance which kind it is. Once you have review progress, sort by fit to see which postings you already cover.
